One film, start to finish.

A photograph becomes a seven-second magazine cover: open the project, brief Codex, mark the title, compare the cuts and export. Waits for the agent and the export are shortened.

Captions
Speed

Step by step

  1. On Home, choose Open project and pick a folder. The folder is the project: the film, its media and every version stay in it, on your disk. The Stillform starter in the film already holds a blank scene, typefaces and reusable cover components.

  2. Open Assets, choose Import files and select the photograph, here grand-teton.jpg. It appears beside the project’s typefaces, ready for the agent to use.

  3. Pick the agent (Codex here; Claude Code or a model you connected works the same) and describe the first cut: what is in the picture, what sits over it and when it comes in. Send it. The agent builds the scene from the project’s components and checks its own render before handing it back.

  4. Play the first cut and pause where it goes wrong. Choose Region, drag a box around what should change and write the note on it: “Let the photograph breathe. Bring the title in half a second later.” Send the marked frame and ask the agent to leave the rest of the film as it is.

  5. The agent moves only the title’s entrance, from 0.08 to 0.58 seconds, and checks the revised frames. Replay the cut, then play both openings side by side. Each revision is kept as a version.

  6. Choose Export MP4. The Studio checks the film, shows the output size and frame rate, and renders the frames on your machine with its own engine: nothing else to install. Save the file wherever you like; here, Stillform.mp4 in the project folder.

Your turn.

Download the Studio, open a sample film and point at something. Connect your model when you are ready.
