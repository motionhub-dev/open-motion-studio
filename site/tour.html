Walkthrough · 1:33

From idea to MP4 in 90 seconds.

One real example: a landscape photo becomes a seven-second animated magazine cover. Describe it, get a first version from the AI, circle what to change and export. Waiting for the AI and the export is cut short.

Speed

Step by step

  1. On Home, choose Open project and pick a folder. The video, its media and every version are saved in it, on your own computer. The folder in the demo already has fonts and cover templates.

  2. Open Assets, choose Import files and pick the photo, here grand-teton.jpg. Once it’s in, the AI can use it.

  3. Pick an AI (Codex here; Claude Code or another model you connected works the same) and describe the first version in a few sentences: what’s in the picture, what text goes on it and when it appears. The AI checks its own frames before handing it to you.

  4. Play the first version and pause where it’s wrong. Choose Region, draw a box around what should change and write a note: “Let the photo breathe. Bring the title in half a second later.” Send it, and ask the AI to leave the rest as it is.

  5. The AI changes only when the title appears, from 0.08 to 0.58 seconds, and nothing else. Play both versions side by side to see the difference. Every version is saved, so you can always go back.

  6. Choose Export MP4, check the size and frame rate, and pick where to save it. It renders on your own computer, with nothing else to install.

Your turn.

Download the app, open a sample video and circle something. Connect your AI whenever you’re ready.
