Describe the film at Home

A 30-second launch film for Ember, a desk lamp that follows the sun. Use the Stage Light look and my two photos in media/. Four beats: desks go dark at 5 pm; Ember lights up; three facts: warms from 6500 K to 2700 K as the sun sets, 14 hours on a charge, 480 g; end on “Ember. Light that keeps time.” 16:9.

Region over the closing tagline, at 28 s

Make this tagline smaller and warm white, and bring it in a beat after EMBER.

Region over the lamp, at 9 s

Push in slowly on the lamp as it lights, and let its glow spill wider across the floor.

Send with both marks

Keep everything else as it is.

Try something bold, then take it back

Try it as a bright daytime film: paper-white background, dark type, no night photos.

Chapters

  1. Thirty seconds for a desk lamp, made from one paragraph.

  2. Say what it's for, its beats, its length and its shape, in your own words.

  3. Browse MotionHub inside the Studio, watch a reel, and add Stage Light.

  4. Import two product photos; the originals stay where they are.

  5. Choose Claude Code, Codex or your own key. The agent writes the film and checks its frames.

  6. Four scenes, thirty seconds, every line on the timeline.

  7. Draw a region on the picture and say what should change there. Two marks, one message.

  8. The tagline and the lamp shot change; nothing else moves. Compare the cuts side by side.

  9. Pick a voice from your own provider; each line is spoken and the captions follow it.

  10. Ask for a bold daytime version, then Go back to before this.

  11. The Studio checks the film and renders it on your computer, narration and subtitles included.

  12. The thirty seconds, with its narration.
